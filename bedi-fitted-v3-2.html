About

Styling for people who want their closet to work for them.

I specialize in personal styling for individuals and creative direction for brands. Whether you're looking to refine your everyday wardrobe, prepare for a special event, or develop a cohesive visual identity for your brand, I work collaboratively to understand your goals and create solutions that are authentic to you.

Services

Closet Edits

A comprehensive review of your current wardrobe to identify pieces that work, eliminate what doesn't, and create a cohesive collection of versatile, mix-and-match basics and statement pieces.

Event Styling

Personal styling services for weddings, galas, red carpets, and other special occasions. I'll help you find the perfect outfit that feels confident and authentic to your personal style.

Travel Packing

Strategic packing assistance for business trips, vacations, and destination events. I create capsule wardrobes that maximize outfit combinations while minimizing luggage.

Brand Campaigns

Creative styling and art direction for brand photoshoots, lookbooks, and campaigns. I develop visual narratives that align with your brand identity and resonate with your target audience.

All services are available for in-person and virtual consultations.
